ANALYTICAL BREAKDOWN

Nine Views. One Unified Narrative.

Categorical breakdown of the 9 visualizations integrated into the Tableau Story workbook.

01 CONSTRUCTOR TEMPORAL

Team Points by Season

Chronological progression of constructor points totals across the 2010–2026 calendar, highlighting dominance eras and regulation changes.

Aggregation: SUM(Points) Season Axis
02 DRIVER STANDINGS

Driver Points Leaderboard

Complete cumulative points hierarchy for all pilots recorded in the dataset, identifying top-tier point accumulators across campaigns.

Aggregation: SUM(Points) Ranked Bar
03 ATTRITION ANALYSIS

Non-Finishes by Reason

Taxonomy of DNF causes, segregating powertrain failures, collisions, gearbox faults, and hydraulics to evaluate operational vulnerability.

Aggregation: COUNT(Dnf) Categorical
04 METEOROLOGY

Non-Finish Rate by Weather

Statistical breakdown of the mean DNF rate across distinct weather conditions (Dry, Wet, Mixed, Extreme Heat), probing environmental risk.

Aggregation: AVG(Dnf) Risk % Rate
05 CONSTRUCTOR HIERARCHY

Team Points Leaderboard

All-time constructor point benchmarks in the dataset, showcasing historical performance gaps between frontline and midfield constructors.

Aggregation: SUM(Points) Cumulative
06 RACE SURGE DYNAMICS

Average Positions Gained

Mean delta between grid starting position and checkered flag finish position, revealing race-day recovery strength vs qualifying bias.

Aggregation: AVG(Positions Gained) Delta Mean
07 TYRE STRATEGY

Starting Tyre Distribution

Frequency distribution of starting compounds (Soft, Medium, Hard, Intermediate, Wet) across race starts, exposing tactical conservatism.

Aggregation: COUNT(Compound) Distribution
08 TRACK TOPOLOGY

Points by Circuit Type

Comparison of championship points scored across permanent road courses, high-speed tracks, and narrow street circuits.

Aggregation: SUM(Points) Circuit Type
09 FINISHING EFFICIENCY

Average Finish Position

Mean classification ranking per constructor team across completed races, providing an unweighted baseline of race execution consistency.

Aggregation: AVG(Finish Position) Mean Finish
SCRUTINEERING & METHODOLOGY

Analytical Scope & Rigor Boundaries

This project represents a descriptive analysis conducted on the course-authorized dataset F1 Racing Project Dataset.xlsx (sheet: race_results).

All metrics reflect descriptive aggregations of supplied historical records. The visualizations illustrate observed statistical associations across weather, tyre, and constructor fields—they do not assert causal mechanics or computational predictive outcomes.
